The Adventurers' Guild of Eisenwald runs on an old terminal program, BurhanQuest. Its guildmaster, the mage Burhan, keeps the guild's secrets behind his admin account — and insists his password 'lives in the guild itself, for those who look closely enough.' You're given a wanderer account and a copy of the program. Get in, look closely, and recover what he's hiding. Wanderer login (usn/password): 'frieren'/'frieren'
