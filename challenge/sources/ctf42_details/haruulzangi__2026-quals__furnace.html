Author: ByamB4

by ByamB4 Reverse Engineering A block came out of the world generator with something smelted into it, but the ore only reads back as noise. There is a furnace in your world. Feed it the right things and the face comes back.

Guessing will not get you there.

A NeoForge 1.21.1 mod that talks to the furnace service. A block was cast with the flag smelted in; it reads back as noise until you dial the one key the furnace accepts.

A block was cast with the flag smelted in; it reads back as noise until you dial the one key the furnace accepts. The transform lives in a bundled native library - reverse it, invert it, and submit the key. Install with TLauncher (no paid account) to watch it smelt in-game, or solve it straight from the jar and the endpoints.
