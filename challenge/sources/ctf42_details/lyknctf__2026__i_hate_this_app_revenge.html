I_HATE_THIS_APP_REVENGE

Reverse #aes-ctr-fixed-key
Difficulty Not rated yet
Mark solved ☆ Save for later
♡ 0

Flag format: LYKNCTF{...}

lizzythecatto

This app is bullying me again. I’ve had enough. I’m going to download their entire image gallery and claim it all for myself.

Oh... Oh no... Instead of getting actual images, all I got was a bunch of weird encrypted text. I wanted a picture, Something real. Something that actually makes sense.

Can you help me decrypt this encrypted file and figure out what it contains? :< I hope there is something cute inside.

Your mission is to decrypt the file, recover the image, and identify the character shown in it. FLAG FORMAT: LYKNCTF{character_name} Write the answer in lowercase. Do not use spaces.

Example: LYKNCTF{lizzythecatto}
